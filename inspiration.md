That list is mostly template sellers, so I'll look at proper design galleries and at sites known for shareable results.

"Coolest" depends on what you're borrowing, so here are three groups.

**1. Competitors to study (for flow and tone, not to copy)**
- **Co-Star** is described as having minimalist black screens and sharp one-liners, and its design is a big reason it spread. Study how little text each screen uses.
- **The Pattern** drops astrology words entirely, which is useful because your audience may not know the jargon.
- **Starcrossed** has a design described as a mix of Co-Star and The Pattern. Study how it moves people from a free reading to a paid one.
- **Astro-Seek and Astro.com** show what to avoid. One review says Astro-Seek's interface is less modern and needs more familiarity with astrological terms.

**2. Galleries for browsing**
- **Full websites:** Awwwards, Godly, Siteinspire, and Httpster.
- **Landing pages:** Lapa Ninja, One Page Love, and Land-book. Land-book lets you filter by page section, such as pricing or testimonials, which suits your paywall.
- **Mobile layouts** (most of your traffic): Mobbin, a library of real shipped app screenshots, free with a paid tier. Screenlane and Pttrns are alternatives.
- **User flows:** PageFlows shows real user flow recordings from successful products.
- **Fonts and colors:** Typewolf and Coolors.
- **Concept ideas:** Dribbble has many astrology UI concepts, including compatibility screens and registration flows. Remember these are mockups, not tested products.

**3. Outside astrology (my suggestion, not from the research)**
Look at how personality-test sites like 16Personalities and Spotify Wrapped present a result and a share card. Your product depends on the moment of reveal and on people posting the result.

**What to look for**
- A first screen that asks for almost nothing (two birthdays) and no signup.
- How the result is revealed, and how much stays locked.
- Pricing and cancel details shown clearly (your edge over competitors).
- A share card that looks good as a TikTok screenshot.

**Two cautions**
- **Awwwards-style sites are often heavy on animation.** That can clash with your requirement for fast results on phones over 4G, so pick the look, not the weight.
- **Take ideas, not assets.** Don't copy anyone's layout, text, illustrations, or branding.
